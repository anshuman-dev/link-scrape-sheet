## 2. Description of Service

TabExcel is a Chrome browser extension that allows users to export their open browser tabs to Google Sheets. The Extension:
- Reads tab information (titles and URLs) from your current browser window
- Creates Google Sheets in your Google Drive account
- Exports tab data directly to your Google Sheets
